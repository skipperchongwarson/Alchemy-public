This is file 3 of 3. Use only when content-system.md doesn't cover the need. This file contains templates and context notes — not instructions. Instructions live in voice-and-writing.md and content-system.md. Run the decision tree in content-system.md first. Come here only for templates.  
  
## Confession / vulnerability  
*Best when you or a client did something brave, failed publicly, or admitted something real. Needs a real moment with a real person — not an abstract lesson.*  
* I stopped [unproductive behavior]. Here's how I [achieved X] with [minimal effort].  
* Why I stopped [common action] (but [unexpected positive outcome]):  
* You might think I'm crazy. But I've spent the last [X days/years] [doing Y].  
* Today I turn [age]. Here are my [same number] lessons.  
* [Timeframe] ago I was [undesirable state]. Now I [desirable state].  
* [Timeframe] ago, [struggle]. Then I [action] that [result].  
* [Pain/hidden struggle] is killing your [goal]. Here's how I [solved/escaped] it.  
* [Number] quick ways I stopped caring about [pain point].  
* [Number] lessons from [number] years of [role]. (I made so many mistakes.)  
* To really understand [audience/subject]… you have to [do X / experience it].  
  
## Contrarian / myth-bust  
*Pair with a client story that proves the contrarian point. A standalone contrarian claim without proof reads as bait.*  
* They say [common belief]. I disagree.  
* They said [X]. I said [Y].  
* [Action/strategy] won't guarantee [desired outcome].  
* Your [obvious thing] isn't the problem. Your [real issue] is.  
* Your [X] isn't failing. It's just [reason].  
* [Audience] don't fail because [common assumption]. They fail because [real reason].  
* "[Common phrase]" is BS. Here's why.  
* You think [tool/idea] is [positive claim]? You're wrong.  
* [Old thing] won't [desired outcome]. [New thing] will.  
* Thinking about [common action]? Here's why you shouldn't.  
* [Target audience] don't care about [popular trend]. All they care about is [real need].  
  
## Client story / scene  
*Lead with the wound, not the product name. Detective narrative — the reader shouldn't know what you're selling until the emotional peak. Never open with a credential or a sprint count.*  
* [Person] was about to [give up] after [X]. Now [new success].  
* [Time reference] I got a [message] from a [type of person].  
* [Specific audience] lose [X] every [time period] because of this [mistake].  
* This [brand/company] reached [milestone] by just [simple/unexpected action].  
* [Audience] — here's an easy tactic to [avoid/fix/achieve outcome]. It'll cost you [minimal investment].  
* [Number] big realizations from just [timeframe] at [event/place].  
* [Skill/Tool] took me from [old situation] to [new situation]. Now I'm handing you the exact [system].  
* [Specific result]. [Specific result]. All thanks to [tool/approach].  
* When [common approach] fails… try [unexpected action].  
* [Doing action] with [no/poor result]? Here's what's actually wrong.  
* [Topic] used to be [old way of doing things].  
  
## Number / list promise  
*Use sparingly. Only when the number itself is surprising — "five tips" is not surprising, "36 platforms" is. Listicles consistently underperform story posts.*  
* [Number] things I'd tell any [specific audience] starting out.  
* [Number] tips I wish I knew earlier (but now apply in every X).  
* [Number] things people still get wrong about [topic].  
* [Number] ways/platforms/strategies to [result] in [context].  
* [Number] reasons to NOT [do X] today.  
* These [number] [tools/strategies] will (actually) [achieve X].  
* [Statistic]% of [audience] [behavior] within [timeframe].  
* [Big %] of [people] are [doing wrong thing].  
  
## Before → after  
*Needs a real number and a real timeline. Without both, use client story / scene instead.*  
* [Timeframe] ago I was [undesirable state]. Now I [desirable state].  
* [Timeframe] ago, [struggle]. Then I [action] that [result].  
* [Skill/Tool] took me from [old situation] to [new situation].  
* [Person] was about to [give up] after [X]. Now [new outcome].  
  
## Tension / stakes  
*The reader has to feel what's at risk — not just understand it. If there's no consequence in the hook, rewrite before anything else.*  
* [Behavior] is the fastest way to [negative outcome].  
* [Audience]. Stop [common practice]. If you REALLY want [outcome], read this.  
* You [do X behavior] and wonder why [negative result happens].  
* You're losing [big result] because you ignore [small overlooked signal].  
* [Specific audience] lose [X] every [time period] because of this [mistake].  
* Your [tool/system] is useless without [essential addition].  
* Your [thing/strategy] might be [positive metric]… but is it [achieving the deeper goal]?  
* [Common assumption] is not the future of [industry]. It's already the present.  
* You've got [tiny time window] to [achieve outcome].  
  
## Question / challenge  
*Only when the question is a pain they're already living. Thought experiments lose. If it sounds like a poll prompt, don't use it.*  
* Want to [big desirable outcome] without [common pain/effort]?  
* Would you trust [X] with [Y]?  
* What they don't tell you about [experience/role/life stage].  
* You want [desired outcome] → start [action].  
* To really understand [audience]… you have to [do X / experience it].  
  
## Pattern interrupt  
*Needs one specific, surprising detail. No vague versions.*  
* This will change how you see [topic] forever.  
* [Tool/feature/idea] is seriously underrated.  
* This [tool/framework] helped me [achieve big outcome].  
* This shouldn't be free. The link to the best [resource]:  
  
## Prediction / future  
*Always pair with a current client story. Standalone predictions are hollow.*  
* [Platform/industry]'s biggest [trend/change] is coming.  
* [Common assumption] is not the future. It's already the present.  
* Your [status quo] will be over soon. And that's the best news you'll hear this [timeframe].  
* [Topic] used to be [old way].  
  
## Social proof / result  
*Only works when the result is shockingly specific. Vague social proof builds zero trust. No real number = use client story / scene instead.*  
* [Specific result]. [Specific result]. All thanks to [tool/approach].  
* [Statistic]% of [audience] [behavior]… and [unexpected/negative outcome].  
* This [prompt/tool/framework] turns [AI/tool] into a [desired transformation].  
* You only need ONE [tool/strategy] to [achieve X]. But make sure it [does Y].  
  
## Story archetypes  
Starting points for post structure — not scripts or templates. Use when you need a structural approach, not a hook.  
**Built around a scene**  
1. Firing scene + AI contrast — stakes through contrast  
2. Churn numbers + CEO silence — BCS entry point  
3. Unmeasurable wins — document: age/job/quote/shift/proxy/timeline/text  
4. Colleague dialogue + messy fear + outcome contrast — disclosure stories  
5. One brand moment + two comparators + reaction — belief installer  
6. Hours waste + tool story + prediction — AI workflow  
7. Quiet crusher fired + poor signals — vague performance  
8. Problem story, offer name never in hook — top-of-funnel  
9. CEO voice + Slack moment — generic pain made specific  
10. Dynamic scene + shift quote — delegation or leadership  
11. Mirror dialogue + tool outcome — imposter or doubt story
