## Professional summary

Product and design leader with 15+ years across startups, product studios, and complex organizations. I help teams turn messy customer and stakeholder evidence into clearer product, service, and strategic decisions, getting into the specific customer, product, service, and organizational details that shape the outcome. I’ve built and led a distributed 12-person Americas design team generating $2.1M in annual billable work, shaped 125 enterprise pre-sales opportunities, and led work from early discovery through prototypes, design systems, and implementation.

Today, through How This Works co, I bring the people making the decision closer to direct customer evidence so they can hear the same customers, compare interpretations, and make the next product or go-to-market call together.
